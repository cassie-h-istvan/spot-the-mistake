# Spot the Mistake

Spot the Mistake is a small project that shows how I check AI work. AI can make small mistakes that are easy to miss, so I look closely and check the details.

Here are 3 examples:

1. The counting mistake — AI said 2 cats, really 3 cats. Counting right matters.
2. The sign — AI said Open, really Closed. Someone might drive there for nothing.
3. The hand — AI drew 6 fingers, really 5 fingers. It shows the AI did not look close enough.

The project page is in `index.html`.
