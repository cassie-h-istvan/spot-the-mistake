Checking AI work

Spot the Mistake

AI can sound sure of itself and still be wrong. Here are 3 small mistakes — what the AI said, what is really true, and why it matters.

How to read it → Each example has the same 3 parts, so you can check them fast: AI said, Really, and Why it matters.
1

The counting mistake

A simple count — gotten wrong.

Count them: 1  ·  2  ·  3 cats — grey tabby, ginger tabby, black cat

✕  AI said
“There are 2 cats in the picture.”
✓  Really
There are 3 cats.
Why it matters
Counting right is a big part of checking AI work.
2

The sign

One word, read the wrong way.

The sign in the picture says Closed — not Open.

✕  AI said
“The sign says Open.”
✓  Really
The sign says Closed.
Why it matters
Someone might drive all the way there for nothing.
3

The hand

A drawing that does not look close enough.

123456

AI drew: 6 fingers All straight out — one too many ✕, extra one circled

vs
12345

Real hand: 5 fingers All straight out ✓

✕  AI said
AI drew a hand with 6 fingers.
✓  Really
People have 5 fingers.
Why it matters
It shows the AI did not look close enough.

What this shows

I look closely, count carefully, and read what is really there — before I trust an AI answer. That is the heart of checking AI work.
